(`plus.unsplash.com`) is a paid licence and was deliberately excluded.

Images were downloaded locally rather than hotlinked, so the site works
offline and does not depend on anyone else's CDN staying up. Each was
requested at the exact ratio measured from the reference storefront:

| Where | Ratio | Size |
|---|---|---|
| Product cards | 2:3 | 800x1200 |
| Homepage rail tiles | 3:5 | 600x1000 |
| Collection cards | 2:3 | 508x762 |
| Hero slides | 12:5 | 1920x800 |
